Dashboard Guide

Pageant: Access the Pageant section to upload your Ramp Walk and Introduction videos.

Master of Ceremony: Access the MC dashboard to record or view MC introductions for shows.

Showcase: View the public showcase of all available talent and pageant shows.

Private Shows: Create and manage exclusive viewing groups. Invite family members and assign custom judges or MCs. Viewing a private show costs 10 tokens.

Judges: Access the Judging dashboard to score performances (if you are assigned as a judge).

Advertisers: Upload and manage video advertisements for shows.

Results: View live and past results of all shows.

Live: Join live broadcasts of ongoing shows.
