Start with the correct shop, then choose its debtor. Each shop keeps its own quotations, payments and balance.

1. Your shops and trades

Use the dashboard's Client Register to open the Debtor Register for the business you are working on. You can keep several shops, including shops with different trades. Use Shop Setup in Output to manage the selected shop's details. To register another shop, return to the dashboard and click Shop Setup. Keep the correct trade on each shop so the appropriate Price List and quotation form are used.

2. Add or reuse a debtor

Open the shop's Debtor Register and click Add Debtor. For a new customer, enter their name, contact number, email and address, then Save. To reuse someone already saved under your account, search Reuse an existing debtor and select their saved details. AIT links their identity to a separate account for this shop, starting at R0.00. Their other shops' balances and transactions stay separate. Click a debtor's name to edit their details. Close hides the form.

3. Create quotations and edit items

Click Quote beside the debtor. For Carpentry and other non-vehicle quotations, Register lists that debtor's saved quotations. Click New Quote to start empty. Select a work item, enter quantity and rates, then Add Item. Add further available items as needed and Save Quote. Carpentry offers predefined work descriptions and the shop's custom items. Other trades have a basic General services item with an editable description. Price List rates are defaults; changing a quotation rate does not change the Price List.

To correct a saved quotation, click its quotation number, then Edit beside the particular item. Change the saved values and click Save Changes. Cancel returns without saving. The quotation keeps its number and other items. The Mechanic trade continues to use its existing vehicle quotation form.

4. Saved quotations and the ledger

Saving a shop-linked non-vehicle quotation records one charge in that debtor's ledger. Editing it updates the same charge. Reopening or saving a correction does not create another charge. This is the current ProTrade account workflow; you do not need to generate an invoice first. Mechanic uses its existing job-card and accounting workflow.

5. Record a payment

Click Ledger beside the debtor, then click Record Payment. For non-vehicle shop accounts, choose Cash, EFT, eWallet, Card or Other. Enter the payment date and amount, with an optional Transaction Reference and description, then save once. The payment appears as a credit. If the payment is already recorded, do not keep submitting it. Existing payment references remain available.

6. Understand the balance

A debit increases what the debtor owes. A credit, such as a payment or deposit, reduces it. The running balance follows transactions in date order. For example, charges of R1,800.00 and R1,473.32 total R3,273.32. A payment of R1,125.00 leaves R2,148.32. Always check the shop, debtor and date range before reviewing a balance.

7. Statements of Account and email

Use SOA from the debtor's ledger to view the Statement of Account and obtain its PDF. Output also lists statements for debtors with saved ledger transactions. In Output, select the shop and an available statement, then Preview. Check the debtor, transactions and balance before printing or saving.

To email from Output, click Email, check the debtor's saved email address, tick the confirmation and click Send PDF. AIT sends only after confirmation. If delivery is not confirmed, check the outcome before trying again. A saved email address is not proof that the address has been verified. Only documents supported by existing records appear; this does not create new invoices.

8. Folders and saving reports

Open Folders from the dashboard. Select one base ProTrade folder and allow browser access. AIT organises reports under Shop / Report Type. The selection is remembered for your account in this browser; permissions may need to be granted again using Allow Access.

In Output, select the correct shop and document, then Preview and Save Report. Files go into that shop's report-type folder. Existing files are not overwritten by repeated saves. Direct saving needs a supported browser such as Chrome or Edge over a secure connection. If it is unavailable, use Download PDF and choose where to keep the downloaded file.

9. Tokens

Use Tokens on the dashboard to review your wallet and available options. Existing Mechanic document actions may charge tokens at the configured price. Opening a register, reusing a debtor, viewing reports and correcting an existing individual quotation item do not add a token charge. The current non-vehicle quotation save does not deduct tokens. Folders and Help do not change your token balance.
