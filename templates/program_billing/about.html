Why Choose Metro Billing?

Designed specifically for the South African billing system, our platform automates utility management for property owners and agents. Simply upload your municipal bill, and our AI instantly extracts your account details, meter numbers, and starting baselines—though complex bulk meters may require brief manual verification. You can configure complex property layouts, from independent units to bulk supplies with multiple sub-meters. Each month, tenants or caretakers can securely submit their readings via a simple email link—eliminating manual data entry.

Platform Costing (Pay-to-Unlock)

You can capture properties and enter all meter readings completely for free. You only pay when you are ready to generate and unlock the monthly PDF statements.

Instant, Professional Statements

With a single click, the system automatically generates three distinct, professional PDF statements:

  • The Tenant MetSoa: A clear, itemized Statement of Account for individual tenants showing their exact utility usage, arrears, and current amount due.
  • The Landlord Ledger: A comprehensive financial overview for property owners, detailing total collected utilities against municipal charges.
  • The Metro Reconciliation Report: A diagnostic master statement that compares bulk meter consumption against the sum of all sub-meters, highlighting unbilled usage or leaks.

Regulatory Protection

Following recent Apex Court rulings, it is explicitly clear that property owners are ultimately responsible for the payment of municipal bills, not the tenants. Our system provides compliance and legally sound billing records to protect landlords from these massive financial risks.

The Prepaid Meter Trap

Prepaid meters are easily tampered with, causing massive, undetected losses for landlords. Furthermore, tenants are often saddled with hidden surcharges by prepaid vendors. Our post-paid readings eliminate these risks entirely.

On-Time, Accurate Billing

Municipalities frequently estimate bills or delay readings. Our system guarantees utilities are based on actual meter readings taken on-time, each and every month.

Dual Statements & Simple Costing

We provide two distinct statements: a utility statement (METSOA) and a comprehensive tenant ledger (SOA). Costing is based on a simple, transparent method rather than confusing municipal tariff tiers.
