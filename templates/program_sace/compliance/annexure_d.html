Assessment Tools & Evaluation Criteria

AIT Professional Development: Reading (LITRE Method)

Formative Assessment Framework

Participants are assessed formatively throughout the workshop via the interactive AIT Platform modules and direct facilitator observation during practical exercises.

Assessment Criteria 1 - Requires Support 3 - Competent 5 - Exceptional
1. Tactile Methodology Application Struggles to map vowels/consonants to the physical palm structure. Correctly maps syllables and uses hand movements to explain concepts. Flawlessly executes palm mapping and innovates new tactile examples.
2. Family Context Integration Uses generic vocabulary disconnected from student backgrounds. Integrates localized words relevant to poor/rural school environments. Builds comprehensive reading exercises entirely grounded in the child's home life.
3. Digital Platform Engagement Did not complete the 18 online video modules. Completed all videos and passed the multiple-choice checkpoints. Completed all videos and submitted high-quality reflection logs on the AIT platform.
Moderation Note: To earn the full CPTD points, a teacher must achieve a minimum score of "3 - Competent" across all three criteria as evaluated by the registered SACE facilitator.
