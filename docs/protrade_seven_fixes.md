2. Removed only the Work Activity action from the Debtor Register. Work history remains accessible from the ledger and is preserved. No migration required. Debtor isolation/history checks: 9 passed.
3. Darkened secondary ProTrade text in the existing templates and dynamic rows, preserving layouts and primary styles. No migration required. Relevant page, catalogue/browser and debtor tests: 13 passed.
4. Admin Hub → ProTrade Pricing reuses `universal_token_tariff` for token prices and adds an append-only `protrade_pricing_revision` audit snapshot. Defaults remain 10 tokens per ordinary quote/invoice/SOA, 25 per BOQ section, existing suggested customer prices and R50/R25/R25 BOQ terms. Admin approval applies prospectively; unchanged paid documents reuse their original receipts. Historical receipt immutability remains enforced. Paystack purchasing/verification is unchanged. The generic tariff page redirects ProTrade edits to the audited approval page; PostgreSQL rejects unaudited updates/deletions. BOQ commercial changes require explicit, internally consistent approval of all amounts together; nothing infers a new operator earning automatically. Help section 9 and document price labels use current approved values. Focused pricing, charging, timing and full-chain migration tests: 20 passed and 14 subtests passed.

5. VAT registration is explicitly recorded in Shop Profile, reusing its existing tax-number field. New rates are controlled, effective-dated and append-only. New quotation snapshots record the treatment, legal basis, rate revision, supply date, exclusive subtotal, VAT and gross total. Inclusive VAT uses `gross ? rate / (100 + rate)` with decimal rounding. Non-vendors cannot charge VAT; standard-rated vendor sales cannot override the approved rate. Legally zero-rated/exempt supplies require a recorded legal basis. Actual eligibility remains the supplier's responsibility. Acceptance no longer creates, emails or charges for an invoice. Explicit Convert to Invoice extends the existing `MechInvoice` model, assigns a unique `INV-` number, preserves the agreed gross total, and freezes the legal document and source financial values. It adds no debtor debit or payment and charges only the approved ordinary invoice price, reusing any historical receipt for that source. BOQ conversion requires paid sections; preparation timing is retained. Legacy financial rows and numbering/ledger functions are not rewritten. Full maintained ProTrade selection: **228 passed, 46 subtests passed**.

VAT baseline and full invoice information were checked against [SARS VAT guidance](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/) and [SARS tax invoice requirements](https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/government/tax-invoices/). Standard rates are read from the database at runtime; the migration seeds the verified statutory baseline. Existing shops deliberately retain an **unknown** registration status until the owner confirms it in Shop Profile; no status is inferred from historical percentages. Confirm each contractor's status and VAT number before preparing new quotations or converting invoices. Issued invoice snapshots retain supplier/customer details, recipient vendor number when applicable, description/quantity, issue/supply dates, serial number and VAT breakdown. Inclusive line prices are allocated to the unchanged agreed gross amount; original quotations remain unchanged.
