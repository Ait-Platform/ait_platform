Welcome to the AIT Digital Evaluation Portal. To test our live attendance and compliance tracking mechanisms, we highly recommend opening the two interactive tools below side-by-side:

  1. Step 1: Open the Facilitator Dashboard (Projector) on your computer screen.
  2. Step 2: Open the Participant App (Mobile View) in a separate browser window or on your smartphone.
  3. Step 3: Enter the Room PIN shown on the Facilitator Dashboard into the Participant App to "Check-In".
  4. Step 4: Click "Start Workshop" on the Facilitator Dashboard to watch the devices sync and track attendance in real-time.
