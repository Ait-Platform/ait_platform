Please Note: This curriculum is meticulously designed around the South African (ZA) national examination standards. However, the core mathematical principles taught here are universally applicable and can benefit students worldwide.

AIT prepares private candidates for the NSC Grade 12 Mathematics examinations. Our rigorous, AI-driven curriculum focuses on building true mastery rather than simple memorization.

Our Core Modules

  • Numbers: Number systems, surds, exponents, logarithms
  • Algebra: Equations, inequalities, sequences & series
  • Functions & Graphs: Linear, quadratic, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric functions
  • Calculus: Differentiation, integration, applications
  • Probability & Statistics: Data handling, probability rules, distributions
  • Geometry & Trigonometry: Euclidean geometry, analytical geometry, trigonometric identities

Support for Private Candidates

To support private candidates (students who failed or want to complete their NSC outside school), we strictly align with the Department of Basic Education (DBE) regulations. Here’s the structured process we follow to ensure legitimacy and academic excellence:

01

Confirm subject eligibility

Check the DBE list of NSC subjects available for private candidates — most core subjects like Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and Languages are included.

02

Register candidates with DBE

Private learners must register through the provincial education department or online DBE portal during the official registration window.

03

Provide accredited material

Offer past papers, study guides, and structured lessons aligned with the CAPS curriculum to ensure compliance with exam standards.

04

Support exam preparation

Integrate timed practice tests, memoranda, and progress tracking in our app to simulate real exam conditions.

05

Guide exam booking

Direct learners to register for the official NSC exam sittings at designated centres — our app provides the preparation, not the final exam.

06

Offer post-exam support

Help candidates interpret results, plan rewrites if needed, and provide pathways to tertiary or vocational opportunities.
