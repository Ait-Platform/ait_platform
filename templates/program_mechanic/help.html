Start with the correct shop, then choose its debtor. Each shop keeps its own quotations, payments and balance.

1. Your shops and trades

Use the dashboard's Client Register to open the Debtor Register for the business you are working on. You can keep several shops, including shops with different trades. Use Shop Setup in Output to manage the selected shop's details. To register another shop, return to the dashboard and click Shop Setup. Keep the correct trade on each shop so the appropriate Price List and quotation form are used.

2. Add or reuse a debtor

Open the shop's Debtor Register and click Add Debtor. For a new customer, enter their name, contact number, email and address, then Save. Search central debtor directory to find a debtor already captured under your user account. Select the debtor and Save to link them to the selected business with a separate account starting at R0.00. If the debtor is new, enter their details once and Save. Contact edits update the central directory and linked account contacts. Each business retains its own quotations, invoices, payments, ledger and statements. Click a debtor's name to edit their details. Close hides the form.

3. Create quotations and edit items

Click Quote beside the debtor. For Carpentry and other non-vehicle quotations, Register lists that debtor's saved quotations. Click New Quote to start empty. Select a work item, enter quantity and rates, then Add Item. Add further available items as needed and Save Quote. Carpentry offers predefined work descriptions and the shop's custom items. BOQ uses the trade catalogue selector to reuse existing Carpentry work items and rates; additional catalogue choices can be added without replacing saved quotation items. Other ordinary trades retain their General services item. Price List rates are defaults; changing a quotation rate does not change the Price List.

To correct a saved quotation, click its quotation number, then Edit beside the particular item. Change the saved values and click Save Changes. Cancel returns without saving. The quotation keeps its number and other items. The Mechanic trade continues to use its existing vehicle quotation form.

4. Saved quotations and the ledger

Saving a shop-linked non-vehicle quotation records one charge in that debtor's ledger. Editing it updates the same charge. Reopening or saving a correction does not create another charge. This is the current ProTrade account workflow; you do not need to generate an invoice first. Mechanic uses its existing job-card and accounting workflow.

Confirm each contractor?s VAT registration status and, if registered, VAT number in Shop Profile. Use the declared supply date and applicable standard, legally zero-rated or exempt treatment. Zero-rated and exempt supplies require a legal basis. Mark rates VAT-inclusive when the agreed amounts already include VAT. A signature accepts the quotation and does not issue a tax invoice. Use Convert to Invoice to issue a separately numbered document with the same agreed gross total and correct VAT breakdown; there is no second debtor debit. Issued invoice snapshots and their source quotation amounts are protected from later edits. Use a new quotation for subsequent financial changes.

5. Record a payment

Click Ledger beside the debtor, then click Record Payment. For non-vehicle shop accounts, choose Cash, EFT, eWallet, Card or Other. Enter the payment date and amount, with an optional Transaction Reference and description, then save once. The payment appears as a credit. If the payment is already recorded, do not keep submitting it. Existing payment references remain available.

6. Understand the balance

A debit increases what the debtor owes. A credit, such as a payment or deposit, reduces it. The running balance follows transactions in date order. For example, charges of R1,800.00 and R1,473.32 total R3,273.32. A payment of R1,125.00 leaves R2,148.32. Always check the shop, debtor and date range before reviewing a balance.

7. Statements of Account and email

Open the debtor's Ledger, then SOA. Choose the reporting period and click Generate SOA. A new statement costs {{ protrade_prices.tokens.soa }} tokens; the same unchanged transactions and period reuse the saved statement without another charge. The saved PDF includes the shop and banking details, debtor contacts, opening balance, charges, payments, running balances and Amount Due.

Open a saved statement to preview or Download PDF free. Use Print / Download PDF / Email to open it in Output. Preview, print, download and email of that saved document do not charge more tokens. In Output, check the debtor's saved email address, tick the confirmation and click Send PDF. Check an uncertain delivery outcome before trying again. Saved statements retain their original contents when the ledger changes; generate a new statement for updated transactions.

8. Downloading reports

Open Output, select the shop and an existing document, then Preview. Use Download PDF to keep a copy through your browser, Print to print the preview, or Email to send it after confirmation. No local folder access is required. Existing quotations, invoices, BOQs and saved statements remain available in the document selector. PDFs previously downloaded remain on your device.

9. Tokens and suggested customer prices

1 token = R1.00. Current Admin-approved charges apply only to new generations. Existing receipts and paid BOQ sections retain their original price. Draft BOQs, edits, previews, corrections and subsequent downloads are free. Generating a complete BOQ from paid sections adds no ordinary quotation charge.
