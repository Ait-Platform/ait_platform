Annexure B

Facilitator Presentation Outline

AIT Professional Development: Reading (LiTRE Method)

Slide-by-Slide Overview & App Integration

This outline provides the paper trail for the digital application, linking the exact facilitator slides to the participant app interactions (T/T).

08:00 - 08:45: Registration & The Reading Crisis

  • Lobby Registration: Projector displays Room PIN. App tracks check-in.
  • Slide 0 (Welcome Activity): Is there a reading crisis? (App Pre-Test: True/False).
  • Slide 1 (Reading State): Current state of Reading in SA (References the 2030 Panel's report).
  • Slide 2 (Live Results): Real-time display of the room's Pre-Test answers.

08:45 - 09:30: The Problem & Realities

  • Slide 3 (The Problem): Identifying the core issue in literacy.
  • Slide 4 (Top Challenges): Participant Reflection - What is your biggest challenge? (App Multiple Choice).
  • Slide 5 (Guessing Game): What percentage cannot read for meaning? (App Interaction).
  • Slide 6 (The Harsh Reality): The 81% statistic revealed.
  • Slide 7 (Catch-Up Approach): Framing the methodology for foundational phase learners.
  • Slide 8 (The English Family): App Verification: The app prompts a discussion: "How will you adapt this story for your specific classroom demographic?".
  • Slide 9 (Introducing the Vowels): Directing focus to the projector for vowel foundation (A, E, I, O, U).
  • Slide 10 (The LiTRE Blending Machine): The Palm as the Meeting Place. App Verification: A digital checklist of the vowels where teachers pair up and grade each other's pronunciation.
  • Slide 11 (Practice Round): Collective practice round utilizing the palm method.
  • Slide 12 (Who is Leaving Home? - Practical 2): Nano Blending Machine. App prompts users to choose between vowels or consonants.

11:00 Onward: Word Building & Application

  • Slides 13-15: Guided word-reading activities, syllable mapping (e.g., Tomato), and worksheets.
  • 14:00 Final Assessment: App pushes the mandatory post-workshop evaluation (Annexure D).
