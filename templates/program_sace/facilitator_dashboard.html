SACE Guide

Live Roster & Active Learning

The platform completely replaces paper registers. The Facilitator Dashboard features a Live Roster that updates instantly. Facilitators use this roster to actively call on specific teachers by name (e.g., asking them to read an article excerpt aloud), ensuring maximum engagement and accountability.

Device Synchronization

When the facilitator changes slides, this Participant App syncs automatically. Teachers cannot skip ahead or fall behind. They are required to interact with activities (polls, reflections, uploads) exactly when the facilitator prompts them.

Airtight Compliance

Because the system strictly logs when a teacher enters the room PIN, how they answer reflection questions, and their final post-test score, it generates an airtight, automated paper trail for SACE CPTD points.

Loadshedding & Offline Contingency

Tech Auto-Recovery: If the Wi-Fi drops, the app safely pauses. Upon reconnection, all devices instantly re-sync to the current slide without any data loss.

Physical Pivot: Our core methodologies (Vowel Hops, A-Z Flashcards) are deeply tactile. If the projector fails, the Facilitator simply switches to their printed flip-book and the physical activities continue uninterrupted.

Paper Assessments: The Facilitator carries physical paper copies of the Annexure D final assessment to ensure certification capture even during a total blackout.

Kinesthetic Memory

Instead of passive listening, the platform enforces physical movement and cognitive mapping (e.g., The Vowel Hops and The Number Map). This proves that the workshop actively uses multisensory learning methodologies.
