ProTrade Workflow Guide

Follow this visual map to understand exactly how a job moves from intake to final billing within the ProTrade system.

1. Intake & Profile Setup

When a new client or job comes in (for example, a vehicle for repair), start here. Use the AI scanner to upload the client's Business Card or relevant documentation (like a License Disk) to instantly generate a new Profile.

Customizing Terms: During setup, you can easily alter the generic Terms and Conditions we supply to perfectly suit your specific trade or business!

2. Quote Generation

Open the Job Card and add specific Labor and Parts lines. The system will automatically calculate totals and generate a professional Quote.

3. Client Approval

From the Job Card, click to send the Quote to the client via WhatsApp or Email. Wait for them to confirm.

Crucial Step: Once the client confirms, change the status to Approved. A popup will appear allowing you to instantly convert the Quote into a working Invoice!

4. Job Completion & Billing

Your team performs the work. Once the job is completed and leaving the shop, change the status to Billed.

5. Debtors & Payment Tracking

Once Billed, the invoice automatically flows into your Debtors Module. Use the Debtors dashboard to track payments, send statements, and manage outstanding balances.
