ProTrade Workflow Guide

Follow this visual map to understand exactly how a job moves from a simple quote to a fully paid invoice within the ProTrade system.

Step 0: Create

Start here! Use the AI scanner to upload the client's Business Card or License Disk. Add your Labor and Parts to instantly generate a digital Quotation.

Customizing Payment Methods: You can select whether you want the client to pay via Bank Transfer, eWallet, or Cash directly on the Job Card!

Pending (List of Quotes)

All created quotes sit in the Pending list. You can email or WhatsApp them to the client. Once the client agrees to the work, click the Confirm button to legally convert the Quotation into a Tax Invoice.

Automated Delivery: Clicking Confirm automatically emails the Tax Invoice to the client as a formal demand for payment!

Confirmed (Debtor Creation & Ledger)

When a Job Card is confirmed, the client's financial Debtor profile is finalized. Once you actually receive the money in hand, open their Ledger and record the payment.

Smart Syncing: Recording a payment in the Ledger automatically updates their balance and upgrades the Job Card status!

Repair Tracker

As you move through these stages—creating quotes, confirming jobs, and logging ledger payments—the Repair Tracker automatically builds a chronological timeline. Both you and the receptionist can easily track the exact status of any vehicle without lifting a finger.
