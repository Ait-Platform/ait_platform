A Simple Journey

TPX connects verified job seekers with verified employers through a secure Employment Passport. Every step is designed to improve trust, transparency and employment opportunities.

1

Register

Create your TPX account as either a Job Seeker or Employer.

2

Build Your Passport

Complete your profile by adding your qualifications, skills, employment history and supporting documents.

3

Verify Information

Upload documents for verification where available. Verified information gives employers greater confidence.

4

Discover Opportunities

Search vacancies or allow employers to discover your Employment Passport through the Talent Marketplace.

5

Connect

Employers review suitable candidates, create shortlists, invite interviews and communicate securely through TPX.

6

Grow Your Career

Your Employment Passport continues to grow throughout your career as you gain experience, qualifications and achievements.

The Employment Passport

Your Employment Passport is your lifelong professional record. Instead of creating a new CV for every opportunity, your Employment Passport evolves as your career grows, giving employers a richer understanding of your experience and skills.

Built for Employers

TPX enables employers to advertise vacancies, search verified candidates, compare Employment Passports and recruit with greater confidence using transparent information.
