HOME — Hands-On Math Education is examined through its existing ten chapter topics across three sections.

  1. Practical: examine the activities and participant instructions in chapters 1–10.
  2. Application / MCQ: examine the questions and application tasks in chapters 11–20.
  3. Theory: examine the explanations, Theory Review material and knowledge checks in chapters 21–30.

Review/retention and assessment/evaluation follow the existing HOME programme. The HOME Board separately exposes the programme materials, both manuals, assessment and monitoring evidence, and applicable certificate evidence.

Auditor examination records evidence for HOME endorsement; it does not award a participant qualification.
