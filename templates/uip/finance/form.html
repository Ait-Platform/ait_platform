Financial record

Amounts are in rand. Expenditure is recorded as paid by an authorised officer; no bank transfer is performed or confirmed. Use signed adjustments only for opening balances or explicit changes to recorded funds. To correct a posted entry, reverse it first and link the replacement.

Publication approves the member-safe purpose and party name. Never enter bank accounts, credentials or personal contact details in those fields. A commitment payment inherits its commitment's publication group. Document permissions remain separate.
