Special Conditions — Preparing, Updating and Filing Your Document

Export Word → Edit and polish → Save as PDF → Upload PDF → Preview → Confirm → File.

  1. Use Export Word for initial preparation from the saved clause records. Edit and polish the document in Microsoft Word.
  2. Add maps, photographs, tables and other supporting material to the Word document in the intended order. Check captions, page breaks, legibility and every clause number. Include all supporting material needed for the Special Conditions in this document.
  3. Save the complete document as PDF in Word. Reopen that PDF and check every page, including maps and photographs.
  4. On Output, select Upload / Replace PDF, choose the PDF and select Preview PDF.
  5. Review the preview, confirm that it is the intended complete document, then select Confirm and File. Uploading or previewing alone does not make it current.

The current confirmed SC PDF is the official Special Conditions document used for Output and Full Lease compilation. Editing clause records does not replace this filed PDF. Existing records and signed documents are preserved.

The Filing Cabinet retains previous confirmed PDF versions. Replacing the current PDF does not overwrite earlier files.

To update a filed document, use Download as Word from the current PDF. Conversion is a starting point: formatting, numbering, tables, vector maps, photographs and scanned pages may not convert faithfully. Scanned pages are not converted by OCR. Check the Word document against the official PDF, restore anything missing, edit it, save a new PDF and repeat the preview and confirmation steps.

Word Import and Compare are no longer required. Include maps and photographs within the SC PDF; there is no separate map upload step. Earlier attachments remain retained as historical records. Ensure the replacement SC PDF contains all supporting material intended for the agreement.

The original signed agreement must never be overwritten.

Verification Before Signing

The user is responsible for reviewing the complete Lease Agreement, including the Core Lease, Special Conditions, maps and attachments, before presenting it for signature.

The user must verify that names, dates, amounts, contractual terms and supporting documents are accurate, complete and satisfactory.

AIT Lease Kit provides document preparation and management facilities. It does not certify the legal accuracy, completeness or suitability of the agreement.

Corrections After Signing

If an error or omission is discovered after signing, the original signed agreement must remain unchanged.

Where legally permissible, the parties may record the correction through a written Addendum identifying the original agreement and the amendment.

The Addendum must be signed where required and retained with the original agreement.

An Addendum does not automatically cure every legal defect. Legal advice should be obtained where necessary.
