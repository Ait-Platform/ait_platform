About Cultural Fire

Talent Begins Here

Cultural Fire is a community-driven youth talent discovery program designed to identify, develop, and showcase young talent across local communities.

Launched to create opportunities for every individual, Cultural Fire provides a structured platform where even children can safely express their talents in areas such as singing, dancing, poetry/speech, acting, comedy, instrumental, and any other cultural performance.

Our Mission

To empower individuals by giving them a stage to be seen, heard, and celebrated— while building confidence, creativity, and community pride.

How It Works

Minors register on the Cultural Fire platform with parent or guardian consent and submit a short video showcasing their talent. Submissions are reviewed, and selected participants are invited to perform in a live community showcase event.

Live events are professionally organised and hosted in local venues, bringing together families, schools, and community members for an unforgettable experience.

Our Vision

To grow Cultural Fire into a leading talent platform across communities, creating pathways from local showcases to regional and national stages.

Community First

Cultural Fire is built on community involvement. Parents, educators, and local supporters play an active role through volunteering, mentoring, and supporting events.

Furthermore, contestants participating in live shows may be required to engage in community-based projects. For example, they may organize or participate in initiatives to raise funds for charitable organizations such as the SPCA or cancer research foundations. This helps instill a sense of civic responsibility and community service in our participants.

The CFI Wallet & Token System

To make participating, supporting, and engaging with the platform seamless, we operate on a secure token system. When you first register for Cultural Fire, you instantly receive an initial bundle of 200 Tokens credited directly to your CFI Wallet.

Tokens serve as the internal currency of Cultural Fire—they can be used to vote for your favorite performers, sponsor segments, or unlock additional platform features. If you need more, you can easily purchase a Wallet Top-Up through our transparent, parity-priced payment gateway.

Safety and Trust

We prioritise the safety and wellbeing of all participants. All learner participation requires verified parental consent, and all content is managed responsibly.

Sponsorship and Partnerships

We work with local businesses and organisations to support youth development while offering meaningful brand exposure within the community.

Join the Movement

Whether you are a learner, parent, school, or sponsor—Cultural Fire invites you to be part of a growing platform where talent is discovered and celebrated.
