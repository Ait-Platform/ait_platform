The Archoney Institute of Technology (AIT) presents this activity for SACE endorsement of the I Learn to Read English Using the LITRE Method.

AIT has generated this secure portal to allow you to seamlessly assign and monitor SACE Auditors and document flows by:

  • generating secure access links for them to evaluate the digital framework,
  • integrating a document tracker allowing you to monitor all forms and auditors,
  • and maintaining intellectual property compliance.

SACE Auditors (Evaluators, Reviewers, etc.): SACE may appoint one or more officials to examine the activity and its supporting evidence. The AIT application refers to these appointed officials collectively as SACE Auditors; Auditor is not necessarily their formal SACE job title.
