The Reading Programme is designed to help you learn English through 18 bite-sized, video-based lessons focused on sounds, word building, and oral practice.

How it works

  • Step-by-step Learning: The course is highly structured. You will unlock the 18 lessons sequentially as you complete them, ensuring you build a strong foundation before moving forward.
  • Learn at Your Own Pace: There are no expiring access windows! You can take as much time as you need to practice, pause, and master the material.
  • Unlimited Review: Once you unlock a lesson, you can replay it as many times as you like to practice your pronunciation. We track your dedication and practice, not your speed.
  • Your Certificate: As soon as you complete the final lesson, you will immediately receive a personalized certificate of completion showcasing your effort and the material you've mastered.
