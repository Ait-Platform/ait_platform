I Learn to Read English Using the LITRE Method

35-step journey: slides 1-31 are the workshop presentation; steps 32-35 are the online interactive components.

  1. Workshop slides 1-31: examine the Workshop Manual for the content participants receive and the Facilitator Manual for delivery notes. The PPP remains available for workshop delivery or fallback use; it is not a separate Auditor examination requirement.
  2. Online workshop steps 32-35: examine the Workshop Online Interaction & Assessment through the existing workshop journey.
  3. Reading course: examine the 18-video Reading Course through its existing journey.
  4. Certification: after examining items 1-9 on the Auditor Board, open the single final Certification recording the Auditor's examination of the Reading endorsement material.
  5. Controlled materials: examine the Workshop Manual, Facilitator Manual, both application forms, timetable and IP pledge reference linked on the Auditor Board. The manuals document the workshop presentation; steps 32-35 are online interactions.
  6. Auditor review: examine the current Reading endorsement material, open Certification, then select Review journey completion on the Auditor Board. Completing the journey notifies SACE Admin and closes the assignment. SACE Admin can download the interaction audit.

The intellectual property pledge accepted when claiming your code continues to apply to these controlled materials.

SACE supplies and controls its own endorsement rubric outside AIT. No SACE endorsement criteria are created or assessed here.

Examination and activity evidence are recorded as you work. You may open controlled materials in any order.
