Annexure E

Facilitator Guide

AIT Professional Development: Reading (LITRE Method)

Facilitator Guidelines & Expectations

1. Pre-Workshop Preparation

  • Ensure the venue is set up for group work (tables of 4-6 educators).
  • Print copies of Annexure C (Worksheets) and ensure sufficient pens/markers are available.
  • Verify internet access. If the school is poorly resourced and lacks internet, ensure all presentation materials are downloaded for offline delivery.

2. Delivery Method (The LITRE Approach)

The facilitator must embody the LITRE method. This means:

  • Do not lecture: Speak for no more than 15 minutes at a time. The rest of the module must be interactive.
  • Use physical modeling: Whenever explaining a concept, physically hold up your hand and point to the palm/fingers. Force the teachers to mirror your movements.
  • Acknowledge the context: Speak directly to the reality of under-resourced schools. Validate the teachers' struggles before offering the LITRE method as a solution.

3. Digital Integration (The AIT Platform)

At the conclusion of the workshop, the facilitator MUST guide all educators to register on the AIT Platform using their smartphones or available devices. Show them how to access the 18 Reading Videos so they can continue their learning and log their final evaluation for CPTD points.
