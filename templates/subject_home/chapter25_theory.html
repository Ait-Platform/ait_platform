Theory Review

Key Concepts

Measurement

Measurement is the process of finding the size, length, weight or amount of something.

Length

How long an object is.

Height

How tall an object is.

Weight

How heavy an object is.

Capacity

How much a container can hold.

Examples

  • Measuring a pencil using a ruler.
  • Measuring water in a bottle.
  • Measuring the height of a chair.

Why Measurement Is Important

Measurement helps us:

  • Compare objects.
  • Build things accurately.
  • Buy and sell goods.
  • Solve practical problems.
