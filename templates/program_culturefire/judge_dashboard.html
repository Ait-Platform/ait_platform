Judging Guide & Rules

  • Score Once: Once you submit a score for a performance, it is permanently locked. You will be warned before submission. You cannot edit scores after they are submitted.
  • Mandatory Scoring: You must submit a score for the current video before the system will allow you to skip to the next video in the show.
  • Point System: Rate each criterion on a scale of 1 to 10 (10 being perfect). The system will automatically tally your final score out of 50.

Criteria Glossary

🎤 Singing / Music
  • Vocal Quality: Tone, breath control, and clarity of voice.
  • Pitch / Intonation: Hitting the correct notes without being sharp or flat.
  • Rhythm / Timing: Staying on beat with the music.
  • Stage Presence: Charisma, eye contact, and audience connection.
  • Creativity: Originality or unique arrangement of the piece.
💃 Dancing
  • Technique: Proper execution of movements, posture, and alignment.
  • Rhythm / Timing: Synchronization with the music's beat.
  • Choreography: Complexity and structural flow of the routine.
  • Stage Presence: Energy, confidence, and projection to the audience.
  • Expression: Emotion and storytelling through movement.
👑 Pageants
  • Confidence / Poise: Grace under pressure and self-assurance.
  • Walk / Posture: Elegance, balance, and physical carriage.
  • Outfit / Presentation: Suitability, fit, and overall styling.
  • Personality: Charm, likability, and natural flair.
  • Overall Impression: The complete package and final takeaway.
🎭 Speech / Other Acts
  • Content & Material: Originality, depth, and creative quality.
  • Execution & Delivery: Clarity, diction, pacing, and overall mastery.
  • Stage Presence: Charisma, emotional connection, and engaging the audience.
  • Structure & Flow: Smooth transitions, logical progression, and dynamic rhythm.
  • Overall Impact: Memorability, resonance, and the whole performance impression.
