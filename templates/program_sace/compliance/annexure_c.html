AIT Professional Development: Reading (LITRE Method)

Workshop Activities & Worksheets

Activity 1: Palm Tracing & Syllable Mapping

Time Allocation: 45 Minutes | Format: Individual Worksheet

Instructions for Teachers:

  1. Trace your dominant hand onto the provided A4 paper.
  2. Label the thumb as the "Root Vowel" (A, E, I, O, U).
  3. Label the remaining four fingers with common contextual consonants (e.g., M, B, P, T).
  4. In the palm center, combine the root vowel and consonants to construct 5 unique syllables.
  5. Write the syllables aloud and practice the tactile memory movement.
Objective: To physically link abstract phonetic sounds to a concrete, tactile map that children can carry with them anywhere.

Activity 2: Word Construction Workshop

Time Allocation: 60 Minutes | Format: Group Work

Instructions for Teachers:

  1. Form groups of 3-4 educators.
  2. Using the syllables generated in Activity 1, construct at least 10 complete words.
  3. Ensure that at least 5 of these words relate directly to the "Family Context" (e.g., Mother, Food, Home).
  4. Present your word list to the adjacent group and demonstrate the physical hand movements used to construct them.
