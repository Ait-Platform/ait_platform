Reviewer Guide: Evaluating the LITRE Digital Platform

Dear SACE Evaluator,

As part of our provider application, we have included access to the LITRE Digital Workshop Platform. This system replaces traditional paper registers and passive PowerPoint presentations with a synchronized, multisensory digital environment.

Because the system is designed to synchronize multiple participant devices to a main projector in real-time, we have built a dedicated "SACE Evaluator Mode" so you can easily review the content at your own pace without needing multiple devices.

1. The Facilitator Dashboard (The Presenter's View)

  • Access: Click the "Test Drive via Simulator" button on the main hub.
  • Evaluator Mode: The simulator disconnects the dashboard from our live servers. You can now use the "Next" buttons to freely read through all presentation slides. Note: To enforce the strict linear flow of the actual app, you cannot go backwards. If you miss something, you must click the Guide (A) tab and restart the simulation from scratch.
  • SACE Guide: The simulator explicitly guides you through how we enforce accountability and our formal Loadshedding Contingency Plan.

2. The Participant App (The Participant's View)

  • Access: Inside the Simulator, click "Push to Participant's Device".
  • Reviewing Activities: You can now freely click through all the interactive polls, the kinesthetic checklists, and the Final Assessment (Annexure D) exactly as a participant would experience them.

3. The Audit Trail (Post-Workshop Reporting)

  • SACE requires a strict audit trail of attendance and competency. On the main Reading Hub dashboard, you can click the "Export Evaluation Report" button.
  • This generates a printable, timestamped PDF ledger proving exactly who attended, how they voted in the live polls, and their final assessment scores.

If you have any questions while navigating the platform, please do not hesitate to reach out to the provider.
