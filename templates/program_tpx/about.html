How It Works

One Passport. Every Opportunity.

Talent Passport Exchange (TPX) is a trusted employment ecosystem designed to connect verified people with verified employers. TPX enables individuals to build a secure digital Employment Passport while giving employers access to reliable information that supports better hiring decisions.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a trusted employment marketplace where people build lifelong professional identities and employers recruit with confidence through transparent, evidence-based information.

Transparency

TPX does not certify people. We verify information where possible and clearly indicate whether information has been self-declared, uploaded, verified or is still awaiting verification.
