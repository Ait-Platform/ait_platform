Consonant Flashcards (10 Mins)

1. Fold: Take a blank A4 page and fold it in half (long edges touching). Fold it in half again, and then a third time to get exactly 8 equal pieces per page.

2. Cut: Repeat this with 3 to 4 pages, then cut along the folded lines.

3. Write: Use a marker to write each letter A to Z on a separate card. You have just made your own physical teaching aid to take back to your classroom!

LITRE Sign Language Game

"Who is leaving home?"

1. The Prompt: The teacher asks, "Who is leaving home?"

2. Assign Consonants: Participant 1 takes 'P', Participant 2 takes 'T', Participant 3 takes 'T'.

3. The Meeting Place: Each participant moves their assigned consonant finger to the middle of their palm to blend with the vowels.

4. Team Up: Together, the three hands combine to spell po - ta - to!

LITRE Word Bank (Open Syllables)

Research supports this kinesthetic method for both English and Indigenous languages.

English
  • ba - by
  • he - ro
  • po - ta - to
  • ba - na - na
Indigenous (Nguni)
  • bo - na (see)
  • ba - la (count)
  • i - ka - ti (cat)
  • u - mu - ntu (person)
This kinesthetic game personifies the letters leaving home, encouraging spelling and collaboration using the palm as the meeting place!
