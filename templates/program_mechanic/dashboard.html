How to use ProTrade & Debtors

ProTrade (Mechanic) allows you to manage your workshop, generate quotes with granular labor/parts control, and issue formal invoices directly to clients.

Debtors Control is a separate, dedicated financial module that links directly to ProTrade. It tracks statements of account (SOAs), interest, and ledger balances for clients who don't pay immediately.

Special Offer!

By using both programs together, you unlock ultimate control over your shop's operations. If you haven't used Debtors before, you must register for it first. Registering for Debtors will give you 200 tokens upfront to control your debtors over time!
