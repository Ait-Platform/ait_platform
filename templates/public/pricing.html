Our Pricing Model

At the AIT Platform, we believe in accessible education. Because we serve learners globally, we use a Dynamic Regional Pricing Model.

How it Works

Our courses are priced based on the economic region of the learner to ensure fair and affordable access. When you select your country during the registration process for any programme, our system will automatically display the exact price in your local currency.

Standard Pricing Baselines

To give you an idea of our pricing structure, here are the standard baseline prices for our most popular modules (prices may vary slightly based on real-time exchange rates and local tax regulations in your jurisdiction):
