How the position is calculated

Opening recorded funds: {{ money(overview.opening) }}. This year's adjustments: {{ money(overview.adjustment) }}.

Recorded funds held: {{ money(overview.cash) }} less outstanding commitments {{ money(overview.outstanding) }} = available funds {{ money(overview.available) }}.

Income and expenditure use the selected March–February year. Recorded funds include the opening balance. Unpaid commitments carry forward and reserve the current year's budget. Adjustments change recorded funds; they are not expenditure. Budget remaining = approved budget minus recorded expenditure minus outstanding commitments. Negative values indicate a shortfall.
