Hands On Math Education

HOME™ — Hands-On Math Education. This innovative course is designed for learners of all ages, with an emphasis on practical, tactile, and evaluation skill development.

The curriculum is structured into three progressive sections spanning 30 chapters, guiding learners from practical application to theoretical mastery:

Section 1: Practical Foundation

10 Chapters • 100% Free

Learn by doing. Gather everyday objects and explore fundamental mathematical concepts through direct observation, comparison, and measurement.

Note: A registered Teacher or Parent is required to review and score your practical submissions in this section.

Section 2: Applied Understanding

10 Chapters • Premium

Bridge the gap between practical exercises and formal theory. Test your applied knowledge through guided multiple-choice assessments.

Section 3: Theoretical Mastery

10 Chapters • Premium

Solidify your understanding with pure mathematical theory, culminating in the comprehensive Final Assessment and Certification.

Note on Premium Access: Sections 2 and 3, along with the Final Examination and Certification, are unlocked via the HOME Premium Bundle. To ensure fair access globally, upgrade pricing is dynamically calculated in your local currency. You will have the option to upgrade after completing the free practical foundation.
