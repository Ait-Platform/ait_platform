Special Conditions — Preparing, Updating and Filing Your Document

Choose either preparation method: enter and save clauses in Data Capture, then use Export Word; or prepare a complete Special Conditions document directly in Word. A PDF upload does not require any saved database clauses.

Select PDF → Upload → Save → Done.

  1. Edit and polish your Word document. Add maps, photographs, tables and other supporting material in their intended order.
  2. Save the complete document as PDF. Check its wording, numbering, page order and supporting material.
  3. On Output, select Upload / Replace PDF, select your PDF and click Upload. A successful private upload is saved as the current Special Conditions PDF immediately. No preview, comparison, review acknowledgement or readiness approval is required.
  4. Use the success message's Open uploaded PDF link to inspect the saved document.

The current SC PDF is the official document used for Email, Open PDF and Full Lease compilation. Saved database clauses are not duplicated in that output. Uploading a PDF does not overwrite clause records.

Earlier PDF versions are retained internally and remain recoverable. Replacing a PDF does not overwrite previous files.

Export Word uses the saved clause records for initial preparation. Download as Word converts the current PDF for further editing. Conversion can change formatting, numbering, tables, vector maps and photographs; scanned pages remain images without OCR. Check the converted document against the official PDF, restore missing material, edit and save a new PDF before uploading it.

Include maps and photographs within your SC PDF. There is no separate map upload or Word Import and Compare step. This Help is informational and does not record approval.

The original signed agreement must never be overwritten.

Verification Before Signing

The user is responsible for reviewing the complete Lease Agreement, including the Core Lease, Special Conditions, maps and attachments, before presenting it for signature.

The user must verify that names, dates, amounts, contractual terms and supporting documents are accurate, complete and satisfactory.

AIT Lease Kit provides document preparation and management facilities. It does not certify the legal accuracy, completeness or suitability of the agreement.

Special Conditions and Addendums

Prepare Special Conditions before signing. An Addendum records agreed corrections or changes to an existing lease, including after signing. On the existing Addendum page, select the related original lease, enter amendment details to create a PDF or upload a completed PDF. Use Open PDF to view, print or download, and Email to send the selected Addendum. Addendum History retains earlier documents and their dates.

Each Addendum should identify the original lease and be signed by the relevant parties where required. AIT provides document management tools, not legal approval. Saving an Addendum does not amend the original signed agreement.

Corrections After Signing

If an error or omission is discovered after signing, the original signed agreement must remain unchanged.

Where legally permissible, the parties may record the correction through a written Addendum identifying the original agreement and the amendment.

The Addendum must be signed where required and retained with the original agreement.

An Addendum does not automatically cure every legal defect. Legal advice should be obtained where necessary.
