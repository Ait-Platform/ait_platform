1. One-Off Registration & Initial Tokens

To join Health IQ, there is a single, once-off registration fee based on your local currency parity. For example, paying 100 in your local currency automatically credits your digital wallet with an initial bundle of 200 Tokens!

2. Pay-As-You-Go Usage

Health IQ operates on a transparent model with no monthly subscriptions. Tokens are deducted only when you actively use the system:

  • AI Lab Report Structuring: 10 Tokens per document
  • Doctor Sharing Links: 5 Tokens per share
  • Timeline Sync: 2 Tokens per event

3. Have a Voucher?

If you were provided a voucher by your healthcare provider, you can redeem it securely on the next step during registration.
