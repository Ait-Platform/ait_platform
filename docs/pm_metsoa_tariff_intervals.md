# METSOA dated intervals and monthly bulk fees

The former effective-date guard rejected an entire meter whenever a selected tariff started inside its reading interval. Monthly fees were attached to that same water row, so rejecting bulk usage also dropped management/refuse and produced a second fixed-fee error.

Consumption is now allocated to dated intervals using elapsed days: opening reading date excluded, closing reading date included. A 15 June to 15 July period has 15 days before July and 15 days from July. A period ending 30 June cannot use a future July rate. Each interval calls the existing electricity or water/sewerage tier builders with its proportional usage and days. Recorded effective-date changes are honoured; no tariff rate/date is rewritten. Identical adjacent pricing is combined to avoid unnecessary rounding differences. Decimal allocation conserves the original consumption; only the existing builders round monetary charges. Fractional quantities are formatted for display without changing calculation precision.

Water Loss Levy and other monthly fixed charges are added once per applicable meter for the reporting month. W_MGT and S_REFUSE are added only to the unique bulk target. Confirmed, audited bulk roots take precedence over legacy bulk-account meter pointers; an account may point to an individual meter sharing that account. With no confirmed root, the existing unique bulk-account pointer remains the fallback. Multiple confirmed roots, missing account association or missing required monthly fees remain incomplete; no meter/link/account record is repaired.

Bulk monthly fees can be priced independently when variable consumption cannot be calculated. Such rows explicitly say monthly fixed charges only and consumption charges unavailable. The report remains INCOMPLETE for genuine missing rates/readings or incompatible intervals. Missing surcharge components do not erase available tier charges. No refund or consumption adjustment is posted; the existing bulk subtraction and nonnegative pricing floor are unchanged.

Tests cover the 30 June 2026 statement, proportional July allocation, earlier-year selection, tier arithmetic, missing preceding rates/surcharges, missing required monthly fees, confirmed-root versus legacy pointer, source-record preservation, estimate status and PDF output. The PDF shows the applicable slice rates and periods in the existing compact layout. Fixtures and PDF artifacts are local only; actual Property 26 production data was not inspected or modified.
