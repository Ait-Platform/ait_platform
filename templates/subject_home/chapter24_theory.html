Key Concepts

Estimation

Estimation is making a sensible guess based on observation and experience.

Estimate

An estimate is not an exact answer. It is an answer that is close to the actual value.

Examples:

  • Estimating how many sweets are in a jar.
  • Estimating how far a building is.
  • Estimating how much water is in a bottle.

Why Estimation Is Important

Estimation helps us:

  • Make quick decisions.
  • Check if answers are reasonable.
  • Save time.
  • Predict outcomes.

Chapter Summary

  • •Estimation is a sensible guess.
  • •Estimates are not exact but should be reasonable.
  • •Estimation helps us predict, plan and solve problems.
