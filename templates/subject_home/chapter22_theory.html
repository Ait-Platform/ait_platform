Key Concepts

Position

Position describes where an object is located.

Examples:

  • Above
  • Below
  • Beside
  • Between
  • Behind
  • In Front Of

Direction

Direction describes movement from one place to another.

Examples:

  • Left
  • Right
  • Forward
  • Backward

Why Position and Direction Are Important

Position and direction help us:

  • Find locations
  • Follow instructions
  • Give directions
  • Navigate safely

Chapter Summary
