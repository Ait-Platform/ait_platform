Build the Blending Machine

Listen to the Facilitator

Play this audio guide while you work through the activity.

The LITRE method begins with a very physical, concrete tool: your own hand. We call this the Blending Machine.

Your palm represents the "meeting place" for letters, while your fingers represent the specific sounds that are coming together.

Practical Activity

Step 1: Take your blank sheet of paper and place your non-dominant hand flat on it.

Step 2: Use a thick marker to trace the outline of your entire hand.

Step 3: Draw a large circle inside the palm area. This is the Meeting Place.

Checkpoint

Confirm that you have successfully drawn your blending machine and showed it to the facilitator.
