How do I use UIP?

Practical guidance for your precinct. Available actions depend on your role.

Command Centre

What needs attention today?

Open the summary tiles to see the underlying register. Review overdue items and assign a follow-up. A summary reflects recorded UIP activity, not confirmation that a service has been completed.

Residents & Properties

How do I register a ratepayer?

Open Ratepayers, choose the add action, and record their name and usable contact details. A person can be registered without an AIT login. Check eligibility and active status.

How do I add a property?

Open Properties and add its address and rates reference. Link the owner or occupier with valid dates, then verify the relationship. Voting eligibility depends on verified dated links, not just an account role.

Operations

How do I log and follow up an issue?

Open Interactions & Issues and log the interaction with its category, location and useful description. Open the issue to acknowledge it, assign a task, record follow-ups or refer it to the municipality. Record completion evidence before resolving it.

How do I record a communication?

Use Communications to record the channel, purpose and outcome. A recorded communication is not proof that an email was delivered.

Service Providers

How do I add a provider?

A manager opens Providers, adds contact details and capabilities, and links authorised provider users where needed.

How do I manage a work order?

Create it from an issue, select a provider and state the scope and service location. Record dispatch. The provider accepts, starts and completes the work; a manager verifies and closes it. Completion never automatically records a payment.

Governance

How do I call a member vote?

Open Surveys and create a poll with a clear purpose, opening and closing times (including UTC offset), eligible relationship and questions. Choose identifiable or confidential responses. The eligible register snapshot controls participation.

How do I send voting invitations?

Open the survey, then Voting Invitations. Check missing email addresses and review the recipients before confirming Send. Each private link permits one response without an AIT login. Use an individual Review resend when needed; a replacement link invalidates the earlier link. Already-voted members cannot vote again.

Accepted means the mail system accepted the message, not delivered or read. Failed and attempted states need follow-up. Test suppressed means no real email was sent.

How do I record a governance decision?

After the survey closes, finalize its results. Use Record decision from these results. Alternatively, use a concluded meeting with recorded quorum. Record the formal decision and follow its existing status process; the poll itself does not automatically approve expenditure.

Finance

How do I create a budget?

Open Finance · Budget. Choose the March–February financial year, category and approved amount. Record the reason for each revision.

How do I record expenditure?

Open Transactions and record expenditure with its date, amount, category and purpose. Link the relevant commitment, provider and supporting document. Review member visibility. Correct posted errors using a reversal, preserving the original record.

What is a commitment?

A commitment is money reserved for an obligation that is not yet fully paid. Link expenditure to it as payments are recorded.

What does available recorded funds mean?

Recorded funds held less outstanding commitments. It is based on UIP records, not a live or bank-verified balance. Member views include approved transparency records only. Financial years run from 1 March to the last day of February and display as YYYY/YY.

Reports

How do I export information?

Open the relevant report or Finance export and choose the period. Check the scope and visibility before sharing. Reports contain recorded information and do not verify bank balances or external delivery.

Administration

How do I change organisation details?

Use Organisation Settings. Keep contact details current and review role access with the responsible administrator.

How do I view the audit trail?

Open UIP Audit and review the relevant event and time. Historical events are retained; confidential votes are not listed as identifiable choices in the audit trail.

What can the AI assistant do?

Use the top-bar AI assistant to draft a notice, communication or minutes, summarise approved text, or explain recorded figures. Review every output. AI does not send messages, cast votes, approve payments or change records.

What are UIP tokens?

AIT credits control organisation AI usage. They are separate from UIP Finance. Managers can view AI & Wallet; platform administrators allocate pilot credits. Each request reserves the configured credits, with a refund on failure. Retrying the same request reference does not charge again. An empty wallet stops AI only.
