1. One-Off Registration & Top-ups

To join Cultural Fire, there is a single, once-off registration fee based on your local currency parity. We operate on a 200% parity model, which automatically credits your digital wallet with an initial bundle of 200 CFI Tokens! You can purchase additional tokens in standard local parity units.

2. Token-Based Participation

Participating in the show deducts tokens from your wallet based on your role:

  • MC (Master of Ceremony): 70 Tokens per assignment
  • Judge: 50 Tokens per show
  • Advertiser (Show Ad): 40 Tokens per insertion
  • Pageant Entry: 30 Tokens per submission
  • General Talent Entry: 20 Tokens per submission

3. The Culture Fire Award

Once you have successfully participated in shows and depleted your token balance to zero, you will be awarded the official Culture Fire Award certificate to commemorate your contributions!
